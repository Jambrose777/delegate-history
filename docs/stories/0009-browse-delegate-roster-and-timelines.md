# 9. Browse the delegate roster and timelines

**Status:** Needs Refinement

#### Acceptance criteria

- Public visitors can browse the WCA-linked delegate roster and each person's historical timeline without authenticating.
- The view displays multiple concurrent active roles independently, such as a Delegate role and a Regional role.
- Missing and approximate dates are visibly distinguishable from confirmed exact dates.
- Read-only public access does not expose private WCA account fields.
